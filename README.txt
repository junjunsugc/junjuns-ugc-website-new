JUNJUNS UGC — FRESH FUSION SCRAPBOOK WEBSITE

HOW TO PREVIEW
Open index.html in Chrome/Edge after extracting this entire folder. Navigation works between six HTML pages.

DESIGN
Inspired by the user-supplied Fresh Fusion palette (#F4CBD3, #F7CD9A, #B8D3EE, #EA9D9B, #808285, #599F9E), vintage paper scrapbook, and retro computer-window portfolio references. All design elements are original CSS, not copied template screenshots.

PERSONALIZE
Replace the YOUR PHOTO HERE art in index.html with your own creator portrait. Add your real projects and video files to assets/ and edit the project mapping in script.js. Existing Medicube video is a sample. Check and configure contact.html email details.

PUBLISH
Upload this folder to a static host, such as Netlify Drop. Before going public, complete Impressum and Datenschutzerklärung and verify all text, links, images and rights.

PAGES
index.html, beauty.html, fashion.html, lifestyle.html, about.html, contact.html.
